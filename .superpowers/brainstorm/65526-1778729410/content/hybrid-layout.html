How should we blend Dev and Design?

Since you do both, how should we organize your work?

A. The Split Toggle

A literal switch at the top (or split-screen). "View as Developer" vs "View as Designer". Tailors the experience based on who is hiring you.

B. Unified Case Studies

A highly visual grid of projects. Clicking one opens a deep dive that shows BOTH the beautiful UI mockups and the technical architecture/code.

C. The Narrative Scroll

A single, long-scrolling storytelling page. It flows through your design philosophy and seamlessly transitions into your technical expertise.
